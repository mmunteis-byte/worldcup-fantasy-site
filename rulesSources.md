# Fantasy Rules Sources

The current rules file uses the official FIFA World Cup Fantasy 2026 How to Play rules supplied from the live FIFA Fantasy game.

## FIFA World Cup Fantasy 2026

Sources:

https://play.fifa.com/fantasy/

https://play.fifa.com/fantasy/help

Used for:

- Squad size, positions, budget, and country limits
- Starting XI and allowed formations
- Captain, vice-captain, and substitution rules
- Transfers and boosters
- Official scoring values for every position
- Direct free-kick and scouting bonuses

The full official How to Play text was checked on June 10, 2026.
